# Executive Summary: E-Commerce Business & Operational Health

This analysis evaluates financial growth, customer retention, and fulfillment operations across **$72.3M in total revenue** and **31 distinct regional markets**.

---

### 1. Revenue Driver Concentration
Net revenue is heavily weighted in primary metropolitan zones. Top-performing territories—led by **Maharashtra ($8.2M)** and **Delhi ($7.1M)**—outperform long-tail markets by more than 4×. However, growth in top markets has hit a plateau, indicating that future expansion requires unlocking underperforming mid-tier markets.

### 2. High First-Order Churn Rate
While initial customer acquisition remains steady, the **30-day repeat rate sits at 18.4%**. Over 81% of buyers do not place a second order within the standard month window. Average Order Value (AOV) remains stable across quarters, confirming that revenue stagnation is a customer retention challenge rather than a basket-size issue.

### 3. Fulfillment Bottlenecks Drive Cancellations
Operations directly dictate customer lifetime value. Fulfillment SLA achievement dropped during high-volume spikes, pushing cancellation and refund rates above targeted thresholds. Operational delays in regional hubs directly mirror regions experiencing low repeat purchase rates.

---

### Key Recommendations
* **Focus SLA Improvements on Strategic Hubs:** Prioritize logistics infrastructure in high-volume regions to safeguard repeat purchase rates.
* **Launch Post-Purchase Re-Engagement:** Introduce targeted 14-day and 30-day lifecycle campaigns to transition single-purchase buyers into repeat cohorts.
