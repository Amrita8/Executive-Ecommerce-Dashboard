# Prioritized Executive KPI Plan

## 1. Net Revenue (Gross Sales - Refunds)
* **What it Measures:** Total realized revenue generated within the specified timeframe.
* **Units:** USD ($)
* **Comparison / Trend:** Month-over-Month (MoM) % change & vs. Monthly Target.
* **Inclusion Rationale:** Serves as the ultimate top-line indicator of business growth and financial trajectory.

## 2. Order Volume & Average Order Value (AOV)
* **What it Measures:** Total completed orders and average dollar amount spent per order.
* **Units:** Integer (Count) / USD ($)
* **Comparison / Trend:** MoM % change alongside 12-week sparkline.
* **Inclusion Rationale:** Distinguishes whether top-line revenue shifts are driven by customer volume or basket size.

## 3. 30-Day Customer Repeat Rate
* **What it Measures:** Percentage of active buyers during the period who previously purchased within 30 days.
* **Units:** Percentage (%)
* **Comparison / Trend:** MoM change vs. rolling 90-day baseline benchmark.
* **Inclusion Rationale:** Acts as a real-time signal for product-market fit, retention health, and organic momentum.

## 4. Revenue Growth by Region / Category
* **What it Measures:** Total Net Revenue split across primary geographic markets or product categories.
* **Units:** USD ($) & % Share
* **Comparison / Trend:** MoM % growth per segment.
* **Inclusion Rationale:** Directly answers *where* growth is occurring across core business lines.

## 5. Order Fulfillment SLA Rate
* **What it Measures:** Percentage of orders packed and shipped within the target delivery timeframe.
* **Units:** Percentage (%)
* **Comparison / Trend:** Weekly trend line vs. 95% internal operational SLA threshold.
* **Inclusion Rationale:** Flags warehouse and supply chain bottlenecks before they impact customer churn.

## 6. Refund & Cancellation Rate
* **What it Measures:** Percentage of orders canceled or refunded post-purchase.
* **Units:** Percentage (%)
* **Comparison / Trend:** Weekly trend vs. maximum acceptable threshold (e.g., 3%).
* **Inclusion Rationale:** Serves as an immediate operational red flag for product quality or shipping errors.
