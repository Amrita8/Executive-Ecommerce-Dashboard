# Draft Notes: Executive Dashboard v1 (Rough Draft)

## Intentionally Rough (Will Polish Later)
* **Visual Aesthetics & Palette:** Chart colors use Tableau defaults without custom branding or alert-color formatting (e.g., highlighting SLA breaches in red).
* **Grid Alignment & Padding:** Container spacing, margins, and grid alignments are unrefined to avoid spending time on layout tweaks prior to feedback.
* **Typography & Titles:** Headers, tooltips, and axis labels rely on standard font sizes and default naming conventions rather than clean executive summaries.
* **Filter Interactivity:** Action filters and dashboard actions are not yet fully wired across all worksheets.

## This is My Answer (Substantive Core)
* **Metric Selection:** The 6 featured KPIs directly answer the executive mandate: revenue direction, regional drivers, and operational risk radar.
* **Single-Screen Architecture:** All key metrics fit within a standard 1366x768 view to eliminate vertical scrolling during executive presentations.
* **Layout Hierarchy:** High-level scorecards occupy the top row, trend breakdowns occupy the middle, and operational health indicators populate the bottom row.
